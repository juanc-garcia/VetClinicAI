# Product Vision

## Product Name
VetClinicAI

## Problem Statement
Veterinary clinics often manage appointments, pet records, vaccinations, preventive care, and medical history across disconnected tools or manual processes. This creates avoidable administrative overhead, missed reminders, incomplete patient histories, and inconsistent care coordination. Pet owners also need a simple, reliable way to access their pet's information and receive timely reminders outside the clinic.

## Vision
Create a centralized veterinary clinic management application that helps clinics operate efficiently while giving staff and pet owners secure, convenient access to care information, reminders, and communication.

## Goals
- Reduce scheduling errors and missed appointments.
- Centralize pet and owner information in one place.
- Improve continuity of care through medical history, vaccination, deworming, and weight tracking.
- Support preventive care reminders for routine treatments and checkups.
- Provide pet owners with a mobile-friendly portal to view their pet's profile and upcoming care.
- Enable rapid emergency contact from the owner experience.
- Improve day-to-day efficiency for reception, clinical, and administrative staff.

## Scope
The initial product will support:
- Appointment scheduling and calendar management
- Pet records and owner records
- Vaccination and deworming tracking
- Weight tracking over time
- Medical history recording
- Preventive care reminders
- A mobile-friendly owner portal for viewing pet profiles and upcoming care
- A one-tap emergency contact action from the owner experience

## Non-Goals for MVP
- Online pharmacy or payment processing
- Inventory management
- Lab result integrations
- Multi-clinic enterprise administration
- Native mobile app in the first release

## Success Metrics
- Reduced missed or double-booked appointments
- Increased reminder effectiveness and attendance
- Faster access to pet and medical records
- Higher owner engagement with the portal and reminder channels
- Fewer manual follow-ups for preventive care
- Improved staff satisfaction with daily workflows

## Stakeholders
- Clinic owners and managers
- Veterinarians
- Receptionists and front-desk staff
- Pet owners
