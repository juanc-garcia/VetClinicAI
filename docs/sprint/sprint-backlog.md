# Sprint Backlog by Sprint

## Sprint 1
### Goal
Deliver the first slice of the MVP by covering core staff operations for appointments and pet records.

### Selected Stories
- Appointment scheduling and conflict prevention
- Pet and owner record management

### Story Points
- Appointment scheduling: 8
- Pet and owner records: 5
- Total: 13

### Implementation Tasks
- Create appointment data model and validation rules
- Build appointment creation and update forms
- Prevent double-booking for conflicting appointments
- Create pet and owner profile data structures
- Implement pet creation and editing workflows
- Link pets to owners and expose staff-facing views

## Sprint 2
### Goal
Extend the MVP with clinical care tracking and the initial reminder foundation.

### Selected Stories
- Clinical care tracking for vaccinations, deworming, weight, and medical history
- Reminder rule foundation for preventive care and appointments

### Story Points
- Clinical care tracking: 8
- Reminder rule foundation: 5
- Total: 13

### Implementation Tasks
- Create care event models for vaccinations, deworming, weight, and medical history
- Implement entry forms for care events and weight updates
- Display care history in chronological order for staff
- Define reminder rules and trigger conditions
- Prepare reminder generation workflow for later delivery

## Sprint 3
### Goal
Complete the MVP by delivering the owner portal experience and the reminder/emergency contact functionality.

### Selected Stories
- Owner portal access and mobile-friendly pet profile views
- Reminder delivery and emergency contact action

### Story Points
- Owner portal access: 8
- Reminder delivery and emergency contact: 5
- Total: 13

### Implementation Tasks
- Define owner authentication and access flow
- Build mobile-friendly owner dashboard and pet profile view
- Display upcoming appointments in the portal
- Implement reminder delivery workflow
- Add one-tap emergency contact action
- Provide reminder status visibility for staff

## Notes
- The backlog is intentionally phased to align with a small team and one-week sprints.
- The MVP remains the highest priority and non-essential enhancements remain out of scope for the planned release.
