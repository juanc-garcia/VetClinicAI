# Sprint 1 Risk Log

## Risk 1: Owner portal authentication may be delayed
**Impact:** High
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** Authentication and access-control decisions are still open and may affect the owner portal timeline.
**Mitigation:** Confirm the preferred authentication approach early and use a simple MVP-ready setup.

## Risk 2: Reminder channel setup may depend on external services
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** Reminder delivery may depend on available messaging providers or configuration support.
**Mitigation:** Prioritize a basic reminder mechanism and document any channel limitations early.

## Risk 3: Emergency contact behavior needs business clarification
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Low
**Description:** The emergency action depends on whether access is always available and which phone number is used.
**Mitigation:** Confirm operating-hours rules and destination number before implementation.

## Risk 4: Privacy and data visibility rules may require approval
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** The owner portal must present clinic-approved summaries, which may require a final review of what data is exposed.
**Mitigation:** Define a minimal, approved data set for the MVP and review it with stakeholders.

## Risk 5: MVP scope may be too broad for a single sprint
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** The selected stories cover multiple workflows and may challenge the team if scope is not tightly managed.
**Mitigation:** Keep the sprint focused on core MVP functionality and defer non-essential enhancements.
