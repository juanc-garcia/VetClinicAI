# Personas

## 1. Clinic Manager
**Role:** Oversees clinic operations and staff performance.

**Needs:**
- A clear view of daily appointments and clinic capacity
- Reliable records for pets, owners, and care history
- Visibility into missed reminders and preventive care follow-up

**Pain Points:**
- Manual scheduling creates conflicts and missed follow-ups
- Information is scattered across spreadsheets and paper files

## 2. Veterinarian
**Role:** Provides medical care and reviews patient history.

**Needs:**
- Quick access to pet medical history and vaccination status
- Clear visibility into current and past health concerns
- Ability to review weight trends and preventive care data

**Pain Points:**
- Incomplete or hard-to-find records slow down care
- Important reminders are easy to miss when handled manually

## 3. Receptionist
**Role:** Schedules appointments, manages client communication, and updates basic records.

**Needs:**
- Simple appointment booking workflows
- Fast access to pet and owner records
- Easy generation of reminder messages

**Pain Points:**
- Repeated phone calls and manual follow-ups are time-consuming
- Scheduling conflicts and missing records create frustration

## 4. Pet Owner
**Role:** Brings pets for care and receives reminders and updates.

**Needs:**
- Easy appointment booking and confirmation
- Confidence that pet records are accurate and available
- Timely reminders for vaccinations and preventive care

**Pain Points:**
- Scheduling can be inconvenient and unclear
- Important care dates may be forgotten
