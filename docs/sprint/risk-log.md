# Release and Sprint Risks

## Risk 1: Owner portal authentication may delay Sprint 3
**Impact:** High
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** Authentication and access-control decisions are still open and may affect the owner portal timeline.
**Mitigation:** Confirm the preferred authentication approach early and keep the MVP flow simple.

## Risk 2: Reminder channel setup may depend on external services
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** Reminder delivery may depend on available messaging providers or configuration support.
**Mitigation:** Prioritize a basic reminder mechanism and document any channel limitations early.

## Risk 3: Emergency contact behavior needs business clarification
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Low
**Description:** The emergency action depends on whether it is always available and which phone number is used.
**Mitigation:** Confirm operating-hours rules and destination number before implementation.

## Risk 4: Privacy and data visibility rules may require approval
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** The owner portal must present clinic-approved summaries, which may require stakeholder review.
**Mitigation:** Define a minimal approved data set for the MVP and review it with stakeholders.

## Risk 5: Sprint scope may exceed team capacity
**Impact:** Medium
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Description:** The combined set of stories may be too ambitious for a one-week sprint if scope is not tightly controlled.
**Mitigation:** Keep each sprint focused on the highest-priority MVP slice and defer non-essential enhancements.
